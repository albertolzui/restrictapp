Privacy & cookies

Last updated: 15 October 2026.

Who is responsible?

Restrictapp is operated by Restrictapp Admin, the controller of personal data collected through this service. Contact olanipekun_albert@live.com for privacy questions or requests.

What we collect and why

  • Accounts: your email and a salted password hash allow sign-in and account administration. We store a hash of your session token, not your password in readable form.
  • Travel planning: passport country, residence, optional passport expiry and dates, route, travel purpose, and optional transit country are processed to prepare your plan. Guest plans are not saved in our account database. Server and hosting request logs may still record technical request information.
  • Saved information: we store a traveler profile, trips and checklist progress only when you choose to save them. We do not request passport numbers, passport scans or payment information.
  • Security: sign-in and deletion attempts use short-lived counters derived from email and IP address using a keyed hash. These are pseudonymous, not anonymous. Hosting providers may also process IP addresses and request metadata for delivery and security.

We rely on performance of the service you request (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)) for accounts and travel planning, and legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) for proportionate security and abuse prevention. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising. Planning results do not make legal decisions about your entry eligibility.

Who processes information?

Render hosts the application and Neon hosts its PostgreSQL database. These providers process information needed to operate the service. Their infrastructure, support, logs and subprocessors may involve processing outside your country or the EEA. Contact us for the current deployment regions, processor agreements and applicable international transfer safeguards; this notice does not promise that all processing stays in the EU.

GOV.UK guidance is fetched by our server using the destination only. Your email and traveler profile are not sent to GOV.UK. The independent travel reference snapshot is bundled with the app. A separately configured travel feed may receive route countries; it does not receive your email or traveler profile. When you follow an external source link, that site's own privacy and cookie rules apply.

How long we keep it

Account information remains while your account exists. Saved trips can be removed individually; deleting your account removes its profile, trips, checklist progress and sessions from the active application database. Sign-in sessions expire after seven days and are removed on subsequent sign-ins; signing out removes the current session immediately. Security counters apply for a 15-minute window; expired counters are cleaned on subsequent authentication or deletion attempts. They may remain physically stored until that cleanup runs.

Provider logs and backups follow the provider's configured retention and may outlast deletion from the active database. Contact us for the current retention periods. Backup restoration must preserve deletion requests. Downloads you create remain on your own device until you remove them.

Your choices and rights

You can use the planner without an account, update your saved traveler profile, remove trips, download account information as JSON, and delete your account through My data in the app footer. Account export excludes password hashes and security tokens.

You can contact us to request access, correction (including email changes), erasure, restriction, portability or to object to processing based on legitimate interests. We may need to verify your identity. We normally respond within one month; lawful extensions or exceptions may apply. You can complain to your local data protection supervisory authority. Find an EU supervisory authority.

Your chosen interface language is stored on this device in localStorage as restrictapp_language (en or de). It is not sent as an advertising identifier. You can change it at any time or clear your browser’s site data.

Cookie policy

The application currently uses one essential authentication cookie. It has no advertising or analytics cookies, tracking pixels, or external font requests.

NamePurposeLifetime
restrictapp_sessionFirst-party sign-in session; set only after sign-in or account creation. HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and Secure in production.Up to 7 days; removed on sign-out or account deletion.

The cookie notice uses sessionStorage under the key restrictapp_cookie_notice_v1 to remember that you closed it in the current browser tab. It contains only the value “closed”, is not an advertising identifier, and is not sent to our server. Closing the notice does not grant consent to tracking. If browser storage is unavailable, the app still works.

If your browser supports service workers, the app stores a public reconnect page and logo in Cache Storage (restrictapp-offline-v2). This lets an installed app explain that it needs a connection. The service worker does not cache account information, API responses, travel advice or the planner page. Clear this storage through your browser’s site-data settings; uninstalling alone may not clear all site data.

Essential sign-in cookies support the account service you request and are not subject to an optional tracking consent toggle. You can block or clear cookies in your browser; sign-in will then stop working. Guest planning remains available. If we add optional analytics or marketing technologies, they must remain disabled until appropriate consent is obtained, with a way to refuse and withdraw it.
