A CLEARER WAY TO PREPAREHow Restrictapp worksPlanning steps, sources, saved trips and useful answers.+
01

Start with your journey

Choose your departure and destination countries. Add your passport country and residence: these can lead to different rules even when two people take the same flight.

Add your travel purpose, dates, passport expiry and one transit country where relevant. You can plan as a guest.

02

Read the reference carefully

Tourism plans may show a passport-based category from TravelRequirements.info. It is an independent reference, not a personalised eligibility decision.

Check each source’s publisher date and limitations. Old, flagged or unsupported records show links instead of a category. Work, study and business plans require further official checks.

03

Confirm with the authorities

Follow the cited government or embassy links. Check visa conditions, passport validity, transit rules and any residence-permit exceptions for your situation.

Optional GOV.UK guidance adds safety, health and entry context; its entry sections generally concern British citizen passport holders. It does not establish rules for every passport.

04

Keep your preparation together

Create an account to save your traveler profile, trips and checklist progress. Changing a saved itinerary’s details resets its checklist so you can review it again.

Download a preparation plan for your records. Tick boxes track your own checks; they do not confirm a visa, permission to board, or entry clearance.

Before you book, and again before departure

Recheck the destination and transit authorities and your airline’s document requirements. Travel rules can change, and residence permits, existing visas and document types may create exceptions this planner cannot resolve.

A few useful details

Do I need an account?

No. You can plan without signing in. Guest details and checks disappear when you refresh; save a trip while signed in to keep them between visits.

What does “publisher check” mean?

It is the date recorded by the dataset publisher for a cited source. The snapshot import date is when Restrictapp imported that dataset. Neither is a fresh independent verification by Restrictapp. Always read the authority’s current instructions.

Does installing the app make it work offline?

Installation gives you a home-screen or desktop icon and a separate app window. Planning, official guidance, sign-in and saved trips need internet. Offline, the app shows a reconnect screen.

How do I manage my personal data?

Use My data in the footer to download your account information or delete your account. You can update your saved traveler profile and remove individual trips. Read the privacy notice for more details.
